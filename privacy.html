Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 2, 2026

Introduction

Fitness Ecosystem ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy applies to all users of the App in every role, including fitness enthusiasts, trainers, and clients. The App is operated by a solo developer and is not a registered business entity. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, profile picture (e.g., from Apple or Google sign-in)
  • Authentication Data: Login credentials and tokens (stored securely; we use Firebase and platform sign-in)
  • User Profile: Age, gender, fitness goals, experience level, body measurements (optional, when you provide them)

Health and Fitness Data

  • Workout Data: Exercise history, sets, reps, weights, rest periods, workout completions, and generated workout plans
  • Nutrition Data: Meal logs, food intake, macro and calorie tracking, meal plans, and photos you submit for meal recognition (e.g., nutrition or body composition photos)
  • Progress Data: Body measurements, progress photos, personal records, and analytics
  • Activity Data: Workout completion, streaks, achievements, and usage of social/challenge features

Health and Wearable Data (Optional)

  • If you connect the App to health or wearable services, we may receive data you choose to share, such as:
  • Apple Health (HealthKit): Steps, heart rate, sleep, workouts, or other data types you authorize
  • Google Health Connect: Similar health and fitness data you choose to share
  • Wearables (e.g., Fitbit): Data synced from connected devices (e.g., steps, sleep, heart rate)
  • This data is used to personalize your experience (e.g., dashboards, insights) and is processed in accordance with your device and platform permissions. You can revoke access at any time in your device or wearable settings.

Payment and Subscription Data

  • We do not store your full payment card details. Payment processing is handled by Stripe and/or the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We may receive and store subscription status, plan type, and transaction identifiers so we can provide the correct features and support (e.g., restore purchases).

Technical Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers (e.g., for crash reporting and analytics)
  • Usage Data: App usage patterns, features accessed, and crash reports (e.g., via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics)
  • Photos/Videos: When you use photo-based features (e.g., nutrition photo analysis, body composition), we process those images to provide the feature; we do not use them for advertising or unrelated purposes
  • Location Data: We do not collect precise location unless you explicitly enable a location-based feature; we do not track your location for advertising

Third-Party Authentication

  • Google Sign-In and Apple Sign-In are used for authentication. The data we receive (e.g., email, name, profile photo) is governed by their respective privacy policies as well as this policy.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App (workout and meal plans, tracking, analytics)
  • Generate personalized workout and meal plans (including AI-based generation and photo-based meal recognition)
  • Sync and display data from connected health and wearable services when you choose to use those features
  • Process subscriptions and restore purchases
  • Track your fitness progress and provide analytics and insights
  • Improve and optimize the App and our services
  • Send you updates and notifications (with your consent where required)
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Ensure security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

  • We use industry-standard security, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and secure storage (e.g., Firebase). Sensitive data is handled with access controls and best practices appropriate to the type of data.

Data Location

  • Data is stored using Firebase and other service providers that may operate in various regions. By using the App, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data as described in this policy.

Data Retention

  • We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and request deletion of associated data at any time through the App or by contacting us. Some anonymized or aggregated data may be retained for analytics and legal compliance.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data with advertisers for ad targeting
  • Use your health or fitness data for marketing without your consent

We May Share:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third parties that help us operate the App (e.g., Firebase for auth and storage, Stripe for payments, cloud and analytics providers). These providers are contractually or legally required to protect your data.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or to protect our or others’ rights, safety, or property
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with notice and continued protection of your data under this policy where applicable)

Your Rights and Choices

Access and Control

  • Access: You can view and access much of your data directly in the App
  • Correction: You can update or correct your profile and preferences in the App
  • Deletion: You can delete your account and request deletion of your data via the App or by contacting us at support@ecofit.studio
  • Export: You may request a copy of your data by contacting us

Account and Privacy Settings

  • Manage notification preferences and privacy-related settings in the App
  • Disconnect health and wearable integrations at any time through your device or the App

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights, including the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information), and the right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or UK, you have additional rights, including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, data portability, and to object to processing. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at support@ecofit.studio.

The App may use local storage and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and support app functionality. We do not use cookies or tracking for cross-app advertising. You can clear app data through your device settings.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We take steps to ensure your data is protected in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law (including, where relevant, standard contractual clauses or other approved mechanisms).
